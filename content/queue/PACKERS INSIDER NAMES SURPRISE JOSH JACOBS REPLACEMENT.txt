My take? It’s Kaleb Johnson’s time. Give him the early downs. Give him the goal line. Let him build on what he did in New England. Mix in Brooks or Strong on obvious passing downs to protect Jordan Love. And see what this young back can really do with a full workload.

Because if Kaleb Johnson can give this offense even a little bit of balance against the Vikings, with Jordan Love throwing to Watson and Golden, the Packers can absolutely win this game. And they might just discover a long-term answer at running back in the process.
